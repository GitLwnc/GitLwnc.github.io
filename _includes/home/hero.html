Research Focus

My doctoral research focuses on desiccation cracking and the hydro-mechanical behavior of variably saturated clayey soils.

I have published five first-author papers in peer-reviewed journals, including Géotechnique and Physical Review E.

My broader research interests include fracture in other porous media and soft materials, as well as the implications of soil desiccation cracking for engineering and geoscience.

## Physics of Desiccation Cracking {#desiccation-cracking}

**What mechanisms underlie the emergence of fascinating crack patterns?**

My work investigates how heterogeneity, interfaces, and stress redistribution control crack initiation and the evolution of connected crack networks in drying porous materials.

## Hydro-mechanics & Fracture Resistance of Unsaturated Soils {#hydro-mechanics}

**How do suction and stress history govern deformation and fracture resistance?**

This research connects suction-dependent fracture resistance with the hydro-mechanical response of unsaturated soils under changing stress and wetting paths.

## Ongoing Direction {#ongoing}

### Predictive Modeling of Drying-Induced Fracture

I am currently extending the experimental work toward predictive modeling that couples transport, deformation, and fracture, including phase-field approaches and coupled hydro-mechanical simulation.

Research Expertise

From experiment to prediction

I combine experimental observation, theoretical analysis and numerical modelling to understand soils and porous materials.

Unsaturated Soil Mechanics

Suction, stress history and coupled hydro-mechanical behaviour.

Soil Desiccation Cracking

Heterogeneity, crack initiation and the emergence of crack networks.

Fracture Mechanics

Fracture resistance, energy-based analysis and failure mechanisms.

Experimental Mechanics and DIC

Controlled testing, full-field strain measurement and multiscale imaging.

Numerical Modelling and Phase-field Method

Ongoing development of predictive models for drying-induced fracture.
